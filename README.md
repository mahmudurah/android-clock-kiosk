# Android Clock Kiosk

Ultra-lightweight, zero-framework wall clock dashboard designed for Android tablets running **Fully Kiosk Browser**.

## Highlights
- **3 Clock Modes (`clockMode`):**
  - `"digital"` — Giant across-the-room digital clock, date, and weather
  - `"split"` — Hybrid **SVG Analog Clock + Digital Clock** side-by-side
  - `"analog"` — Giant horological **SVG Analog Clock** + large date & weather
  - `"auto"` — Automatically uses `"split"` on `maria-rainbow`, `swiss`, and `braun`, and `"digital"` on the rest
- **Kid-Friendly Learning Features for Maria (`maria-rainbow` & `maria-space`):**
  - Color-coded **Hour hand** (matches `1–12` numbers) and **Minute hand** (matches outer `05..55` minute markers)
  - Plain-English **Time in Words** (`• ten past eleven`, `• quarter to eight`)
  - Time-of-day personalized greeting (`☀ Good morning, Maria!`, `★ Sweet dreams, Maria`)
- **10 Built-in Themes (`theme`):**
  1. `maria-rainbow` — Maria's Time Teacher (Warm cream, coral & sky-blue learning dial)
  2. `maria-space` — Maria's Starlight Galaxy (Cosmic navy, pink/cyan hands, gold stars)
  3. `swiss` — Swiss Railway SBB (Gallery white, jet black ticks, red lollipop second hand)
  4. `braun` — Braun Atelier Dark (Matte charcoal, warm ivory, Braun yellow second hand)
  5. `oled` — Pure OLED Night (True `#000000` black, soft silver & blue accent)
  6. `amber` — Retro Nixie Amber (Warm vacuum-tube orange on black)
  7. `terminal` — Phosphor Terminal (CRT green monospace)
  8. `eink` — E-Ink Paper (Matte stone paper & serif ink)
  9. `nord` — Nordic Frost (Arctic slate & frost blue)
  10. `red-night` — Bedroom Red (Zero night-vision glare)
  - Plus `"auto"` day/night switching (`dayTheme` / `nightTheme`).
- **On-Screen Controls:**
  - Swipe **left / right** or tap bottom-right `◐` to cycle themes.
  - Tap bottom-left `◷` (or tap the analog clock) to switch between **Digital**, **Split (Analog + Digital)**, and **Analog**.
